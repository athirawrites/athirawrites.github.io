I use the pen name Athira Rhina to share my erotic stories; in other places, you may recognise me as AthiraWrites or just Ath.
This name was born on the Paradevo forum, where I was called "Athena" by mistake so many times that I shortened it to Ath.

For disclosure, I am a female devotee of disabled men - and I am disabled myself (I do not have a visible disability).
Now that I have shared this with you, if you are not comfortable with disabled men being objectified in erotica, then these stories might not be for you.

All characters in my stories are over 18, have agency, and give consent; there is passion and curiosity, but pity is not included.
