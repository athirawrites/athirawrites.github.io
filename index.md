Welcome!

I write steamy romance and erotica where one or more characters have a disability.
I published my first story over eleven years ago, before I had a longer break.
There is still a lack of disability representation in erotica, but mainly, I just want to entertain you with hot stories, and hopefully,
you will find something you like.

First is the second edition of "First Touch," where I have fixed typos and a few problems with the tense.
I will be re-editing and touching up a few other shorts that were previously published on my old website, before publishing new material.
Drop by later for more news.
